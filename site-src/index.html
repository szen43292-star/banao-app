How to install

Up and running in a minute

Windows

  1. Download Banao.exe and open it from your Downloads folder.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This shows up because Banao is new and not yet signed with a certificate.
  3. Banao opens in its own window. Right-click its icon in the taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar to open it quickly next time.

Banao uses Microsoft Edge, which comes with Windows 10 and 11, to show its window. Your projects are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Banao. To remove Banao, delete Banao.exe and that folder.

SHA-256 of Banao.exe, to check your download{{EXE_SHA}}

Mac, Linux and Chromebook

  1. Download Banao.html and move it somewhere you’ll find it, like your Documents folder.
  2. Double-click it. It opens in your web browser and works without internet.
  3. Bookmark it, or keep it in your dock or on your desktop, and always open it in the same browser.

Your projects are saved inside that browser. Clearing the browser’s site data removes them, so download your finished websites and software as files to keep copies.

SHA-256 of Banao.html{{HTML_SHA}}

Android

  1. Download Banao.apk on your phone and tap it when the download finishes.
  2. Android asks whether to allow installing apps from Chrome (or your Files app). Turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. Banao appears with your other apps. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.

Files you save in Banao go to Documents › Banao, and the share menu opens so you can send them on WhatsApp or save them to Drive.

iPhone (advanced)

  1. On a Windows or Mac computer, download Banao.ipa and install Sideloadly or AltStore.
  2. Connect your iPhone with a cable, drag Banao.ipa into Sideloadly and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. On the iPhone, open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, trust your Apple ID, then open Banao.

Apple lets apps installed this way run for 7 days before they need installing again. The App Store version will not need this.

Questions

Before you download

Is Banao free?

Yes. Banao and everything you make with it are free, and the websites and software you create are yours.

Do I need internet, an account or an API key?

No. Banao works fully offline on your device. There is no sign-up and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Internet is only used, when available, to load nicer fonts in the websites you preview.

Why does Windows show a warning?

Windows warns about new programs that are not yet signed with a paid certificate. Banao is safe to run: click More info, then Run anyway. You can also compare the file’s SHA-256 with the one shown under How to install.

Where are my projects saved?

On your own device. On Windows they are in your user folder under AppData\Local\Banao. In the Mac and Linux file, they are saved in the browser you open it with.

When are the Android and iPhone apps coming?

The Android app is next, followed by the App Store version for iPhone. When the Android app is ready, its download button appears on this page.

How do I put my website online?

In Banao, click Download to save your website as one file. Rename it to index.html and drag it onto Netlify Drop. You get a free public link in about a minute.
